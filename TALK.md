## Beyond the Data Swamp: Building Your Semantic Observability Mesh

Most engineering teams are currently drowning in a "data swamp". We collect more logs, metrics, and traces than ever before, yet when a critical system fails, we still feel blind.

The problem isn’t a lack of data; it is a lack of shared understanding. This session explores how to move away from disconnected silos toward a Semantic Observability Data Mesh.

We will share a journey of transforming raw, expensive telemetry into a powerful and unified asset. We will demonstrate how ClickHouse provides the high-speed engine needed to process data at scale without breaking the budget.

However, the real magic happens when we give that data context and meaning. We will move beyond standard OpenTelemetry conventions to show how you can build and extend your own custom semantic layers.

Finally, by using Weaver to validate these schemas, you can ensure your systems all speak the same language.

You will learn how to turn a chaotic sea of noise into a searchable, reliable source of truth that reveals the root cause of problems in seconds.
